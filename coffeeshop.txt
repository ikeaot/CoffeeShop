Dont worry, we have a tutorial for that (VALUES WILL BE UNTOUCHED)
In the First Day, you'll be given:
- Coffee Beans (g): 20x
- Water (Oz)      : 10x
- Milk (Oz)       : 15x
Credits / Coins   : 5 Coins

There are 3 types of drinks:
// Each drink gives different rewards corresponding to the ingredients //
- Espresso  (8 creds): 3x Coffee Bean
- Americano (7 creds): 2x Coffee Bean, 1x Water
- Latte    (10 creds): 1x Coffee Bean, 2x Milk

On day-1, you will need to complete 5 orders.
TYPE in the right amount of ingredients to complete
 w/ (0) == No Ingredient
Each 2 days after day-1 will increase the amount of orders 
 you need to COMPLETE by +1

You may encounter insufficient ingredients, for that you need to buy.
 Go back to the menu -> TYPE: 3, to the Grocery Shop

// Each ingredient needs specific amount of coins //
 You will get coins from completing orders.
- Coffee Bean (1 gr): 2 coins
- Water (1 Oz)      : 1 coin
- Milk (1 Oz)       : 3 coins
 Spend wisely.
    // INSUFFICIENT INGREDIENTS == UNABLE TO COMPLETE ORDERS //
